MIDI

Incoming (default zones): ch 1 → upper, ch 2 → lower, ch 3 → pedal, each sent on to its out channel; CC 12–20 drawbars of the zone's manual (pedal: CC 12–15), CC 1 mod strip, CC 11 expression, CC 64 sustain, pitch bend (zone screen, SEL + KNOB 4: bend the pitch or rotary kick/tutti).

Controls

KNOB 1–4the four parameters of the screen; hold SEL (or Shift) for the second row
ENV / LFO / EDITdrawbars 1–4 / 5–8 / 1, 3, 4 and 9 — in the MIDI zones: the screen's switches
FXthe effects: a row per manual (ENV/LFO/ARP = UM/LM/PM): drive, vibrato or tremulant, delay send, reverb send; SEQ: the manual through the rotary; SEL + knobs: percussion/chiff/detune, delay feedback and time, the room. FX again: back
GLOthe MIDI zones: ZONE → CLONE → HARMONIC → CHORD; HOME leaves
SELECT / ALGOmanual (zone) UPPER/LOWER/PEDAL / model 1–8, in every screen
PRESETSthe drawbar list (vintage 1–36, pipe, free reed, then USER 1–16; the pedal its own list); tap SAVE to store the drawbars into a USER slot
HOMEon the drawbars: the MIDI mixer, 16 channels, CC 7 (ENV/LFO/ARP/SEQ = rows); again: back
SEL + GLOthe lights of keys and buttons: OFF / DIM / FULL, and the dim level
Setupshold SEL + one of the 16 white keys (F3…G5): recall a performance setup; hold SAVE + the key: store it
ARP / SEQvibrato (tremulant) of the manual on/off / coupler II/I
PLAY / RECRotary brake / rotary slow↔fast (SEL + REC: the manual through the rotary or not)
OCT− OCT+octave of the FM-1's keys
KeyboardZ…/ = F3…A4, Q…U = A4…G5 (piano layout), mouse and touch
StripsMOD: rotary slow / fast (organs; BEND ROTARY: slow / brake / fast), bellows (reeds). PITCH: bend ±2 semitones (organs with BEND ROTARY: up = rotary kick, down = tutti); always sent out

The FM-1 platform has no reachable touch strips: on the device their jobs come in as MIDI CC 1 and pitch bend. MIDI out is USB only.
