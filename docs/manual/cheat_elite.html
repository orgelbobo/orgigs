OrGigs Elite at a glance

A three-manual organ, effects rack, MIDI router and SEQTRAK remote for the M-VAVE SMK-37 Elite.

package SMK-37 Elite_674150
GPL-3.0-only
Sound84 free-running tone generators, no voice stealing; 16 models: eight vintage and combo organs, four pipe organs (baroque, cathedral, renaissance, romantic), four accordions
Playing3 manuals (9 + 9 drawbars, 4 pedal stops) on 8 knobs, fader 1 and 16 pads; 5 pad layouts; coupler; sustain or expression pedal; chord memory; wheels with their own targets
EffectsPer manual: drive, vibrato / chorus or tremulant, rotary on / off, delay and reverb sends. Shared: two-rotor rotary (4 cabinets, 2 your own), delay, 4 reverbs, an insert slot (chorus, wah-wah, auto-wah, flanger, phaser, ring mod, amp), master EQ and compressor
Memory16 performance setups (one per pad), a drawbar list of 75 registrations + 16 user slots; kept after power-off; backup over USB
MIDI3 zones, cloner, harmonic mapper, chord memory; a 16-channel mixer (volume, pan, reverb, delay, mute, solo); the Yamaha SEQTRAK's 11 tracks on the pads (YST)

1Rules that hold everywhere

Keysonly play, on every screen (C3–C6, with velocity); OCT− OCT+ −2 … +3

KNOB BANKthe next manual: UPPER → LOWER → PEDAL (when you let go)
KNOB BANK + FADER BANKHOME: upper manual, model 1, P1, coupler off, layout A
FX · GLOBE · PARAthe FX pages · the MIDI screens · the mixer; again: back to the drawbars
ARP / PATCHon the FX and MIDI screens: the previous / next page
PLAY · RECrotary brake · slow ↔ fast (SCALE + REC: the manual through the rotary or past it)
SCALE + pad · BT + padrecall · store performance setup 1–16

Pads 1–11choose the track (the SEQTRAK follows, and the other way round); SCALE + pad: mute
Pad 12the action pad: held, it pulls a SEQTRAK effect up, the harder you strike the faster
< > · pad 16the previous / next sound · the sound browser (K1 category, K2 sound, loads when the knob rests)
K1–K8cutoff, resonance, attack, decay, volume, pan, reverb, delay of the track; SCALE + K1–K4: the DX track's FM
Faders 1–3the SEQTRAK's touch sliders: FX LEVEL, HIGH PASS, REPEATER
SEQ · KNOB BANKsynth track mono / poly / chord · the manual that plays the track

From anywhere: hold < + > and turn K4 (category) or K5 (sound) of the track the manual plays.

Avoid MIDI loops: never route the Elite's output back into the Elite. For a 5-pin DIN synth behind a MIDI host, switch pad 8 (TRS) on the zone screen.

OrGigs is designed around the Elite. A compact edition runs on the M-VAVE FM-1: the same organ, effects and MIDI zones on four knobs and twelve buttons. Both read the same backups.
